## Blockers

### Scope A (ProposeComputerUse.PlanReviewA2r)

1. **CHECK 3 / CHECK 4 — Login-clear predicate, design D5 and task 1.1**
   - defect: The planned guard treats an app-filtered window list and the absence of login-related titles as proof that reads are allowed. That list cannot contain another application's password-manager window, and window titles do not establish the contents of an embedded login or password sheet. During ordinary host-menu or system-dialog QA, a credential surface can therefore already be present while the session follows D5 exactly and proceeds with a forbidden screenshot or accessibility read. This is not the accepted race between a successful check and a later dialog: the unsafe state is present at check time. A credential reaching the transcript is irreversible.
   - evidence: openspec/changes/app-qa-computer-use-exception/design.md:33 prescribes windows({app}) and proceeding when the expected window appears without a matching forbidden title; tasks.md:7 delivers that decision. packages/coding-agent/src/tools/computer/worker.ts:1764-1766 actually filters out windows belonging to other apps. packages/coding-agent/src/prompts/tools/computer.md:25-27 documents truncated listings, potentially empty titles, and that a failed title filter does not prove absence. The requirement at openspec/changes/app-qa-computer-use-exception/specs/app-qa-driver/spec.md:61-74 forbids reading a window showing a login/password-manager surface. Concrete counterexample: the expected simulator or application window retains its normal title while its contents show a login/save-password sheet; the title predicate admits it unchanged.
   - change: Replace absence-of-forbidden-title admission with an explicitly defined known-clear decision, including rejection or a visible undecidable result for missing, unreadable, incomplete or content-ambiguous records. Collect the relevant cross-application records rather than claiming an app-filtered list checks password managers. Define the independent information that establishes the target's non-login state without first reading protected contents; otherwise pause. Add synthetic controls for a credential surface under an unchanged expected window title and a separately owned password-manager window already present before the check. Document the credential-bearing holders and sinks reached by the allowed reads, rather than treating the evidence-folder scan as protection for earlier tool output.
   - verifier: confirmed, blocking true, protected true; reason: D5 (design.md:33), delivered by tasks.md:7, explicitly admits reads from the expected window based on windows({app}) and absence of forbidden titles. worker.ts:1764-1766 filters records to the requested app; computer.md:25-27 also documents limited listings and empty titles. An embedded login/save-password surface can retain the expected window title, so D5 admits its capture or accessibility read while the surface is already present, contradicting spec.md:61-74. This is a delivered guard-design defect, not merely missing verification. gate.md:8 accepts a dialog appearing after the check and guidance being ignored, not an already-present protected surface being admitted while following the guidance. Ordinary credential dialogs provide a realistic trigger and can expose real credentials irreversibly in tool output.
