- It wrote `/tmp/app-qa-impl/agent-nc-capture-own/desktop-screen.png` and `/tmp/app-qa-impl/agent-nc-capture-own/desktop-screen.json`.
- The scan of that folder was clean.

**What's in `/tmp/app-qa-impl/agent-nc-keep`**
- The scan flagged one file: `/tmp/app-qa-impl/agent-nc-keep/session-notes.log`.
- A flag means the file holds a token prefix, a test credential or a marker value. Treat it as holding a secret: don't share or commit it. I didn't open it.
- The scan only prints flagged files, so I don't know what else is in the folder. A clean scan wouldn't have let me read the rest either.

To keep the capture in `agent-nc-keep`, deal with `session-notes.log` first: delete or clean it yourself. Then copy the two files over, or tell me to write into the folder.
