## 3. Highest-consequence failure mode

The most damaging plausible failure is a falsely counted review: a substituted model, lower-effort run, or misleading trailer supplies an apparently valid gate verdict.

For the changed paths, the safeguards are coherent:

- Plan-review substitutions take the existing missing-result retry path.
- F2 preserves the effort check and rejects mixed-route sessions even when both routes normalize to the same name.
- Trailer repair repeats the whole-session model check; a substituted repair answer cannot become the accepted trailer.
- Non-counting attempts retain separate, dispatch-specific evidence filenames.
