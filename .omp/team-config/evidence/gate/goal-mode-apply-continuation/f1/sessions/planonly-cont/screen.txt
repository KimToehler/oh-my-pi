 conservative to be.

 I'm going with freezing the cached widget, since it's cheap and protects the cache from callers. I'm also adding an exported clearWidgetCache() for resets, and still weighing whether unbounded growth needs a bound.

 I'm weighing whether unbounded growth is acceptable, leaning toward treating memory growth with distinct ids as a residual risk that clearWidgetCache() can release. I'm also leaving empty ids unspecial-cased. For the
